# Leveraged positions

{% hint style="warning" %}
Leveraged positions are a **future addition**. Not in v1.
{% endhint %}

A future version of the protocol will add **native support for one-click leveraged borrowing**.

## The mechanic

A user deposits collateral, borrows against it, and the borrowed funds are immediately reinvested as additional collateral — all in one transaction.

## Why it's worth doing

* **Better capital efficiency** for borrowers who want it.
* **Simpler UX** than doing the loop manually.

## What it requires

* **Substantially more risk per position.** Leverage amplifies both upside and downside.
* **Clear, prominent risk disclosure.** Users need to understand the magnified exposure before opening a position.
